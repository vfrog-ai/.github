# Hey, we're vfrog 👋

**We collect human experiences.**

We build **[Azimov](https://vfrog.ai/)** — egocentric data collected by real workers in real workplaces, on high-end multisensor devices. It's training data for the robotics and computer vision teams teaching machines to work alongside people.

---

### 🎥 What We Do

Professional workers wear our multisensor devices during real shifts, and we turn those recordings into licensable egocentric datasets with full provenance.

- **Real work, not staged demos** — footage comes from live facility maintenance jobs, with hospitality, food packing, construction, and line work coming next.
- **Multisensor capture** — synchronized cameras, IMU, hand tracking, and head pose on a lightweight wearable. [See the device specs](https://vfrog.ai/#device).
- **Indexed episodes** — every episode comes with action descriptions, so you can find the moments you need.
- **Ready to train** — delivered as a LeRobot-compatible package, or as raw MP4 plus sidecar JSON, with a Hugging Face dataset card and calibration files.
- **Clean provenance** — every worker signs a consent and IP assignment before their first shift. Data is anonymized before delivery, with no audio.
